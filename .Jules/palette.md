## 2024-08-02 - Stateful Local Storage & Accessibility for Single Page Apps
**Learning:** In purely client-side static monolithic single-page applications, placeholder features like "Wishlist" are often left as fire-and-forget triggers (e.g., simple toasts). Elevating them to stateful, toggleable elements with robust `localStorage` persistence and active styling (such as shifting outline styles to solid filled rose/red states) provides a dramatic increase in perceived product maturity and user delight. Additionally, icon-only buttons (such as the `×` character or sharing emojis like 💬/📷) are completely screen-reader unfriendly unless they are explicitly accompanied by descriptive `aria-label` attributes to define their exact contextual purpose.
**Action:** In future UX/accessibility tasks, always inspect placeholder components for quick statefulness and audit close/emoji buttons to guarantee `aria-label` is present.
