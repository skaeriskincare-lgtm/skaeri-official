# Palette's UX & Accessibility Journal

## 2026-08-06 - Interactive Wishlist Toggle States
**Learning:** Persisting wishlist items in local storage is a standard UX best practice, but real-time visual feedback (changing from empty ♡ to a filled ♥ with high contrast gold brand-themed border and background) on button interaction in product detail modals prevents user confusion, confirms action success immediately, and boosts overall user delight.
**Action:** Always provide instant, state-synchronized toggle styling for add-to-wishlist or favorite buttons, and preserve their state across dialog opens/closes via localStorage.
